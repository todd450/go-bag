Option A at a glance

What sets it apart: the smallest change to the screen. The chip row stays as it is and only the words change: the count is true, and a line names what's missing. Things already in the kit are never named, so "four pain relievers count once" is only implied by "1 of 3".

Tagging: opens inside the Next steps row, with no review screen. An item the app can't place is left to tag by hand on the Kits tab.

New components: none.

Cost: about 2 days of the week's UI work. The rest of the week goes to the scoring, tagging and test changes every option shares. Fits the appetite with room.

What it gives up: green is believable again, but you can't hold the row against the closet, because it doesn't say which essentials it found.
