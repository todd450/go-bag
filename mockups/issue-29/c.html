What's new in this frame

Where-label (new component)
Every ticked essential names the bag to open to check it: Cash · Hall closet go-bag, Fire extinguisher · Basement bin. Green can be checked against the closet, not just believed.
Question in the row (new component)
An item the app can't place sits inside the row it would fill, with each answer's points on the button. One tap saves one tag and the row re-reads in place. There's no separate review list.
Essential pill (new component, shared with B)
Ticked pills for what's there, dashed pills with points for what's missing. Four pain relievers show as one pill that says it counts once.
What has to be trusted here
That green means ready, and that a tap saved only what it said. The row names each essential, where it is, and what each answer is worth before it's tapped.
Found while building this
House fire is an evacuation, so today it counts go-bags and car kits only. The storyboard's extinguisher is in Basement bin, which House fire wouldn't count. The where-label is what makes that visible. The build has to choose: count home kits for House fire's Fire safety, or show the extinguisher as missing with "In Basement bin, which House fire doesn't count".
